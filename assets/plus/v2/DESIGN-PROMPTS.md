# Original Maydan Plus art briefs

These are normalized design briefs for the original image_gen artwork, not an extraction of hidden generation metadata. No branded third-party characters or images were used. The supplied gold-frame handoff is retained as the permanent sahra frame; membership uses a separate trophy frame.

Shared direction: premium soft 3D / clay collectible craft; Maydan ivory #FFF6E5, plum #30203F, teal #06768B, purple #8540B5, gold #FFBF36. Original characters, clean mobile silhouettes, warm readable materials. No text, logos, UI or watermarks baked into the illustrations. Portraits and tables have transparent exteriors; circular frames have transparent exteriors and apertures. Each category is separately composed rather than a crop of a cover.

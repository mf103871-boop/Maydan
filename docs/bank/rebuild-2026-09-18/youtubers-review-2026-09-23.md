## Review method

Sources prioritize official profiles, original publishers and institutions; interviews and publisher book descriptions cover a few creator and literary facts. No current follower totals or current officeholder claims are used. Historical appointments are explicitly dated.

Questions and accepted alternatives were read for factual scope, ambiguity, spelling, answer leakage and suitability for a verbal quiz. Wording is at most 22 words and canonical answers at most six words. No topic occurs more than twice in a tier.

قبل ما يطق / القنبلة
Survival and elimination order now determine placement lexicographically. Passing
many prompts cannot outrank a player who survived longer. The podium accepts a
separate rankScore while retaining remaining hearts as the visible score, so
zero-heart eliminated players no longer all display second place. Ordinary
score-based games and genuine ties retain their existing behavior.

Explicit exhaustion now creates a fresh seeded permutation, avoids repeating
the last visible word immediately, and guarantees a changed order for pools
of at least three items. For one item a repeat is unavoidable; for two items
avoiding the immediate repeat takes priority over a changed permutation.
Saved sessions record the shuffle cycle. Resume replays the seeded completed
cycles before restoring the cursor, preserving the current and future queue.
Existing sessions without a cycle field restore using their original first
permutation. The existing visible repeat notice remains.

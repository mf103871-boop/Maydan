Your group, your next game. Maydan brings seven Arabic party games together for drawing, guessing, trivia and shared laughter, on one phone or in private online rooms.

DRAW AND GUESS LIVE
In Ersomha (ارسمها), one player draws while the others guess together. Two players cooperate against the clock; three to eight compete individually. Take turns drawing, reveal synchronized hints and watch the canvas update live.

TRIVIA AND MORE
Badeeha offers 78 packs with 3,120 questions covering knowledge, puzzles, pictures and audio challenges worth 200 to 1,000 points. Form teams, connect clues and choose when to use your team powers.

Keep playing with Before the Beep, Mamnoo!, On Your Forehead, Fabraka and Meen Fina. Race the timer, describe words, invent convincing answers and vote together. Ersomha, Fabraka and Meen Fina support private online rooms across devices. Fabraka supports two players in its suitable modes; Meen Fina requires at least three.

BRING YOUR FRIENDS
Find friends by name or friend code and connect after a request is accepted. Send text messages and voice notes, make audio calls and create invitation-only groups for up to 12 members. Share a room invitation in chat and jump into a game. Friends, messaging, voice notes, calls and groups are free and do not require Maydan Plus.

Participant photos and speaking indicators stay with your call, with movable call bubbles during play. Switch between Games, Friends, Store and Settings using the bottom menu.

Make your profile your own with a photo, cover, biography, earned titles and badges. Preview a recording before sending it. Control notifications and message previews, mute conversations, block accounts, report content and delete your messages or account.

FREE PLAY AND MAYDAN PLUS
Ten Badeeha packs are always free, and every other game includes one complete free match. Joining a room by code is free. Maydan Plus unlocks every Badeeha pack, unlimited matches and unlimited room creation through an auto-renewing monthly or annual subscription. Local prices and billing periods appear before purchase. Manage or cancel renewal in your App Store account and restore purchases from Maydan settings.

Single-device games can be tried without an account and work offline after their content is ready. Apple or Google sign-in is required for social features, online rooms and linking a subscription. Live drawing, messaging, calls, online rooms and purchases require internet access. The games and interface are in Arabic.
